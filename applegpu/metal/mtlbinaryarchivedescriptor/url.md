## Discussion

You can use this method to load a binary archive you created with an [MTLBinaryArchive](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/mtlbinaryarchive) instance’s [serialize(to:)](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/metal/mtlbinaryarchive/serialize(to:)) method.
